About Ayurveda

Ayurveda is India’s ancient science of life that promotes harmony between body, mind, and soul through food, routine, herbs, and lifestyle practices.

Why Choose Ayurvedic Living?

Ayurveda focuses on prevention and natural healing by understanding your unique body constitution. It uses dosha-based diet, daily routines, herbal support, yoga, pranayama, and seasonal living.

Ayurvedic Principles

Understand the core foundation of Ayurveda for better health and wellness.

The Three Doshas

Vata = movement & airiness, Pitta = heat & transformation, Kapha = stability & nourishment. Each person has a unique doshic balance.

Pancha Mahabhutas

The five elements are Space, Air, Fire, Water, Earth. These elements form the doshas and influence body functions, emotions, and digestion.

Agni & Ama

Agni is digestive fire. Strong Agni means good health. Ama means toxins or undigested waste. Ayurveda focuses on boosting Agni and reducing Ama.

The Three Doshas Explained

Vata Dosha

Light, dry, cold, mobile. Associated with movement, creativity, quick thinking, but can cause anxiety, dryness, and irregular digestion when imbalanced.

Best: Warm, oily, grounding food

Pitta Dosha

Hot, sharp, intense. Associated with digestion, intelligence, ambition, but can cause acidity, irritability, and inflammation when aggravated.

Best: Cooling, calming, less spicy food

Kapha Dosha

Heavy, slow, cool, stable. Associated with strength, immunity, calmness, but can cause lethargy, weight gain, and congestion when excessive.

Best: Light, warm, stimulating meals

Ayurvedic Diet & Nutrition

Balanced eating according to dosha, season, digestion, and mindful habits.

Vata Diet

Eat: Warm soups, rice, wheat, milk, ghee, sweet fruits, cooked vegetables.

Avoid: Cold drinks, dry snacks, raw salads, excess fasting.

Pitta Diet

Eat: Coconut water, cucumber, leafy greens, sweet fruits, milk, rice.

Avoid: Spicy, fried, oily, sour foods, excess tea/coffee.

Kapha Diet

Eat: Light meals, ginger tea, warm water, green vegetables, millets, honey.

Avoid: Heavy meals, sweets, dairy excess, oily foods, daytime sleep.



Seasonal Eating (Ritucharya)

Summer: Cooling foods, coconut water, cucumber, melons.
Winter: Warm soups, nuts, ghee, herbal tea.
Monsoon: Light cooked food, boiled water, avoid stale food.

Kitchen Pharmacy

Turmeric: immunity & anti-inflammatory
Ginger: digestion & cold relief
Cumin: improves digestion
Cardamom: freshens breath & supports gut health

Food Combinations to Avoid

Ayurveda advises avoiding certain pairings such as milk + fish, fruit + milk (some cases), and very heavy incompatible meals that weaken digestion.

Key Ayurvedic Recommendations

Eat According to Hunger

Eat only when the previous meal is properly digested. This supports strong Agni and prevents toxin accumulation.

Mindful Eating

Avoid mobile, TV, stress, or rushing while eating. Sit calmly and chew food well for better absorption.

Warm Food Principle

Favor warm, freshly cooked meals over cold, stale, or overly raw foods—especially when digestion is weak.

Meal Timing

Have your heaviest meal at midday when digestive fire is strongest. Keep dinner light and finish by 7–8 PM.

Home Detox Knowledge

Gentle detox may include warm water, light khichdi meals, herbal teas, and rest. Panchakarma should be done under expert guidance.

Daily Consistency

Regular wake-up time, meal timing, movement, and sleep create stability and improve physical and mental balance.

Lifestyle & Daily Routine (Dinacharya)

Simple daily habits that support digestion, immunity, sleep, and emotional balance.

Morning Rituals

Tongue scraping, oil pulling, drinking warm water, gentle stretching, and mindful breathing help start the day with balance.

Sleep Hygiene

Sleep early, avoid heavy late-night meals, reduce screen exposure, and create a calm bedtime routine for better rest.

Yoga & Pranayama

Practice Anulom Vilom, Bhramari, and gentle yoga. Different doshas benefit from different intensity and pacing.
